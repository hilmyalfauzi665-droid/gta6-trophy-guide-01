Roadmap to Platinum

Difficulty about 4/10, roughly 200 hours to platinum (PowerPyx). No mission replay, cheats disable trophies and saving, and free-roam continues after the story — so almost everything can be finished after the credits. Only one trophy is possibly missable: Positive Mental Attitude (#28). Work the stages in order.

  1. Stage 1 — Story first, 60–80 hours

    Play the story across its 5 chapters and do the optional missions as they appear. Scan vehicles and study animals through binoculars as you go — 200 of 277 vehicles and 100 of 255 animals count toward 100% later. Do the first meditation in Chapter 1 (Lucia, balcony at Jason's house, the morning after “Down to Earth”) — it may be required for #28, and that is unconfirmed, so do it to be safe.

  2. Stage 2 — 100% completion & the big counts, ~100 hours

    After the credits, the 100% checklist appears under Progress > Stats > 100% Completion. It has about 35 requirements and is roughly 60% of true completion: 70 of 80 challenges, 200 of 277 vehicles scanned, 100 of 255 animals studied, 300 of 397 stunts, 30 unique race wins, 15 of 18 card sets. Clear the counts in this stage — they are the long grind.

  3. Stage 3 — Strangers & Freaks and cleanup, ~10 hours

    Finish the 8 stranger mission strands, then sweep the remaining miscellaneous trophies: partner robberies, vaults, showroom and bowling-alley moments, money and property. The platinum pops with the last trophy.

Missable: only #28 Positive Mental Attitude is flagged as possibly missable (see its guide). Every other trophy is finishable in post-story free-roam, and nothing on the 100% checklist is missable.
